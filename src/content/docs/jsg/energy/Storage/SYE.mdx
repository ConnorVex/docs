JSG offers a few ways to store the energy that you produce. This energy is valuable as it can not only power your machines, but also your Stargates!

There are 2 types of storage that JSG offers:
- Reusable Storage - Energy storage that can be used over and over again.
- Single use storage - Energy storage that can only be used once.
