Capacitors are the primary energy storage device of JSG. They can store an enormous amount of Forge Energy. You can right click a capacitor to see its exact level, or just look at the indicator on the block itself. Capacitors are also portable, and have a myriad of uses beyond just being a power bank.

<center>
## Power Source
</center>
The Capacitor stores Forge Energy, which means it can be used to store power from generators that produce FE like Mekanism’s fission/fusion reactors. It can be used to also power machines that need FE as the capacitor can also transfer energy. They can also be used to charge items in your inventory if necessary as well!

<center>
## Stargate Upgrade
</center>
Capacitors are a must have for Stargates. They raise the internal energy buffer by storing more energy and allow you to be able to dial further than without them. Every Stargate (except Uni) has 3 capacitor slots.

<center>
## Creative Capacitor
</center>
Creative Capacitors are an item that can only be obtained in creative mode or with commands. It has infinite charge and is basically the 'Thor'  of all capacitors. If a creative capacitor is placed in a world, then the only way that thing is getting deleted is by someone in creative mode.
