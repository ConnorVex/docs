If you want to calculate the cost of dialing between 2 specific gates, you may have a heart attack or a stroke or even both due to the complex nature of the calculations. Fear not however! The JSG team has given us wisdom!

You can use the [JSG Energy Calculator](https://justsgmod.eu/utils/energy/) to figure out how much energy is needed to dial your destination. Just put your Dimension Config and Stargate Config into the tool, edit the data(**gate type, gate position and dimensions etc, etc**) and BOOM! You now see how much FE you need to complete your dial!

This tool could be useful in helping you determine whether a gate is in range of your current gate or you will need to get closer.
