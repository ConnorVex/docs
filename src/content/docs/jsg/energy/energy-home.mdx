Energy is one of the most important parts of this mod. After all you cannot dial a stargate if you don't have any energy!

JSG uses unified energy units - FE (Forge Energy). Any mod that uses FE as an energy system is compatible with JSG. Any cable, generator, etc can be used with JSG with **hardly** any issues.
The most used and reccomended mods to use with JSG are:
- [Mekanism](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/mekanism)
- [Immersive Engineering](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/immersive-engineering)
- [Thermal Expansion](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/thermal-expansion)
