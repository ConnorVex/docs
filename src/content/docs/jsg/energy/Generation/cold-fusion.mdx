Cold Fusion is currently the only way to generate power **RELIABLY** without the use of other mods. The CFR (Cold Fusion Reactor) is in the [Dial Home Device (DHD)](/jsg/stargates/dhd) and is the primary power source of the stargate.

It is important to note that the CFR **ONLY** supplies power to the stargate. It cannot be used to power other devices, or even other gates as each gate can only have a single DHD at any given moment.

Cold Fusion Reactor requires fuel to operate - liquid refined naquadah, which is consumed during reactor operation. Entire operation of the reactor is completely independent and self-sufficient - as long as Stargate energy buffer can store energy, reactor is active and will only shut down if fuel runs out or Stargate energy buffer is full.

[The CFR can be upgraded to produce more energy for less Liquid Refined Naquadah.](/jsg/stargates/dhd/#upgrades)
